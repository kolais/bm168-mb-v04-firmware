A working list you can drive, to feel the feedback rules instead of reading them. Turn with the arrow keys or the buttons, push with Enter, go back with Esc, and click rows to touch them. Switch between Full and Lite and between the three sound levels; the panel on the right names each answer as it happens.

Things to try: turn past the end of the list (Bump), tap "Android Auto" (Deny), open "Phones" and push another phone (Connecting in place, then the chime), hold an arrow key (fast turning shortens the ring travel, then jumps).

The sounds here are synthesised in the browser from the recipes in the Feedback section; the firmware ships them as pre-rendered samples. The strip of buttons under the panel is lab chrome, not part of the launcher.
