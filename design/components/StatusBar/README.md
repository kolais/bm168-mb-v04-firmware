The frame's own things, the same wherever they appear: the status in the top band, and the Settings cog on Home. Until 2026-11-22 they stood with a Back button in a 112px rail down the driver's side. At the bench the owner moved the status to the top, kept the cog where it was, and took Back off the screen.

**The consumer provides** the time (`name-28`, `ink`) and the state of Bluetooth.

- **Status**: Bluetooth and the clock, at the right of the top band, on every launcher screen, in a fixed 164px (`name-28`, `ink`; the mark 28px). Bluetooth has three states, as the owner asked on 2026-11-23: no mark while it is off, the rune while it is on, the rune with a short line at either side while a phone is connected to it. On this unit Bluetooth is on only while a phone is being connected or paired and off while CarPlay runs, so the mark is seldom there. There is no Wi-Fi mark: a running CarPlay session is told by Home's chip, which then shows the CarPlay sign and the phone's name: a ring open at the right around a solid play mark, in `ink` like the Bluetooth mark (the shape the owner asked for, from the mark a phone shows in its status bar; drawn on the icon set's grid). Without a session Home has no chip: that no phone is connected is on the tiles ("Guest" and "No phone for profile N" still show, behind the hollow ring). Marks for "radio on" and for "link up" were tried at the panel that day and read as unpredictable. The band has no fill of its own; a screen's title, chip or field share it and end 24px before the status.
- **Settings** is on Home only: a 60px circle, `surface-raised` with `edge-light`, under the tiles on the driver's side, its outer edge on the line of the last tile and of the status (`space-5` from the screen's edge). It is the last stop of the knob on its side and takes the amber ring like a pill; the ring stays 19px clear of the tiles. A finger gets 12px around it.
- **There is no Back button.** The knob has its Back key. By touch, a list ends in a "Back" row, the waiting screen and text entry have "Cancel", and Home has no way out on the screen (a "car" button like CarPlay's may come).
- Nothing here is `blue` or a status colour.
- "Steering side" in Display settings moves the cog, and the side the tiles stand on, to the other side.
