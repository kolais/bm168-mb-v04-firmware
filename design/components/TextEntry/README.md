Text entry for names, in two forms that swap by themselves. Opened with the knob, it shows the dial; opened by touch, it shows the keyboard. Touch the screen while the dial is up and the keyboard replaces it; turn or push the knob while the keyboard is up and the dial returns. The text is kept across the swap.

**The consumer provides** the field's title, the current text and a maximum length (24 here).

- **Field**: in the header band beside the title, `row-32` on `surface`, with a caret.
- **Dial**: characters on a full circle, A at the lower left running clockwise to Z, with the five functions along the bottom: numbers, case, delete, space, OK. The characters stay put and the amber ring travels round them; the centre repeats the focused character at `display-48` and names a function. Turn to move, push to type. The dial wraps.
- **Keyboard**: QWERTY, keys 103 × 60 with `space-2` gaps, `surface-raised`; shift, delete and the bottom row are `surface`; "Done" is filled `ink`. A second layer holds digits and punctuation.
- The knob's Back, or "Cancel" after the field, leaves without saving. "OK" on the dial and "Done" on the keyboard save.
