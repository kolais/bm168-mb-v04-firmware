The same tiles and pills in Full (left) and Lite (right), to show that the two modes share every position, size, colour meaning and word.

- **Full**: `glass` and `glass-raised` fills over the baked wallpaper, `edge-light`, and `focus-glow` around the amber ring.
- **Lite**: opaque `surface` and `surface-raised` on plain `ground`, `edge-light`, ring only.

The wallpaper in this preview is a stand-in drawn with CSS; the real one is a single image baked at build time. Motion differences are listed in the Two modes section.
