The connecting screen: a spinner around a phone icon and one line of text, shown for as long as the unit is trying a phone (no time limit of its own; "Still trying …" on a repeated attempt). Back returns to Home.

**The consumer provides** the phone's name. The line is `title-36`, `ink`, centred, one line; truncate the name, never the verb.

The arc is `blue` on a faint `outline` track, 136px across, 5px stroke, one turn per second. It turns smoothly in both modes. When the unit gives up ("Couldn't connect to …") the arc goes and the track stays. Nothing on this screen takes knob focus.

The same screen serves pairing ("Choose “…” in your iPhone's Bluetooth settings", with the unit's name) and the software update ("Updating. Keep the ignition on", without the phone icon).
